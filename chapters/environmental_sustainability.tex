\chapter{Environmental and Sustainability Analysis}
\label{chap:environmental}

An instrument built for environmental monitoring should itself leave a small
footprint, so this chapter considers the problem the OpenStat addresses
and its impact over its life.

Antiretrovirals are not fully removed by South African wastewater treatment
works and reach surface water at up to
\SI{102}{\micro\gram\per\litre}~\cite{vogt_arv_sa_waters}, far above the
\si{\nano\gram\per\litre} levels at which efavirenz harms fish~\cite{env_robson2017}.
Monitoring is limited by the cost of laboratory analysis, which a low-cost
portable instrument helps to lower. The components for the prototype cost
R2\,226 including VAT (Appendix~\ref{app:bom}).

The largest savings were made in the hardware itself. The AD5941 integrates all the elements discussed in Section~\ref{sec:potentiostat_fundamentals} into one
millimetre-scale package~\cite{ad5941_ds},
keeping the board small and the part count low. The SPEs, the only
consumables, were cleaned with ethanol and reused throughout
testing, which added to the variation seen in Section~\ref{sec:fc_deviation}. Table~\ref{tab:env_lifecycle} lists the remaining measures.

Charging the \SI{18.5}{\watt\hour} cell through the linear charger draws about \SI{29}{\watt\hour} from a grid emitting
\SI{0.960}{\kilo\gram}~CO$_2$e/\si{\kilo\watt\hour}~\cite{env_dffe_gef2024},
roughly \SI{28}{\gram} CO$_2$e per charge or \SI{0.02}{\gram} per scan, so
operating emissions are negligible.
% 29 Wh: 5 Ah x 3.7 V = 18.5 Wh; linear TP4056 efficiency about 3.7 V / 5 V = 0.74;
% USB adapter about 0.85 (assumed); 18.5 Wh / (0.74 x 0.85) = about 29 Wh.

The main remaining improvement is in the power chain: boosting the battery to
\SI{5}{\volt} and then dropping to \SI{3.3}{\volt} in a linear regulator discards about a third of
the stored energy, and a \SI{3.3}{\volt} step-up/step-down regulator would extend the run time by roughly
\SI{50}{\percent} with the same cell. Safety, cost and societal
considerations are discussed in Appendix~\ref{app:ga3}.
% 50 %: (5 V x 160 mA / 0.9) / (3.3 V x 160 mA / 0.9) = 1.52.
